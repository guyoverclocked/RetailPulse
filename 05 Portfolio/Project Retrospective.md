# Project retrospective

## Why

A candid retrospective proves engineering judgment and converts unfinished ideas into a credible roadmap.

## Answer briefly

- What result changed your original belief?
- Which simple baseline was hardest to beat?
- Where could leakage or bias still enter?
- Which component would fail first at 10× scale?
- What did the optimizer teach that forecast metrics did not?
- What did you cut, and why was that correct?
- What are the next three experiments in priority order?

Separate product limitations, data limitations, and engineering debt. Link major choices from [[Decision Log]].

## Alternatives

A generic “lessons learned” list is easy to write but weak. Tie each lesson to evidence or an explicit decision.

## Done when

The repository has a tagged release, a limitations section, and a prioritized next-step backlog.
