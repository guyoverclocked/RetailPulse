# Resume and interview story

## Why

The project earns attention only when you can state its scale, rigor, decision impact, and ownership precisely.

## Resume formula

`Built [system] for [scale/problem], using [key methods], achieving [measured result] against [baseline], and deployed [operational proof].`

Draft two bullets only after results exist. Possible evidence includes 1,115 stores, 30-day horizon, backtest improvement, P10–P90 coverage, API latency, test count, or simulated optimization improvement. Never invent a metric.

## Interview arc

Problem → constraints → leakage-safe evaluation → baseline → champion/challenger evidence → uncertainty → staffing decision → deployment/monitoring → limitation and next experiment.

## Alternatives

A technology-heavy story may pass keyword screens but performs poorly in interviews. Lead with decisions and evidence, then name tools.

## Done when

You have two quantified bullets and can deliver 30-second, 2-minute, and 5-minute versions without notes.
